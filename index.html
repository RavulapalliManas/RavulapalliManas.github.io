Research interests

Where the work sits

Structure in learned representations

What a predictive network can and cannot represent, given only egocentric observations. The quotient law predicts when place fields should repeat across identical rooms, and map quality degrades monotonically with the order of the landmark symmetry group.

Developmental pretraining

When representational structure becomes usable during training. Acquisition, retention, loss, and repair of a capability in continued pretraining, tested under a preregistration protocol with predictions filed before launch.

Mechanistic interpretability

The gap between what a model represents and what it uses at query time. In-context binding failures localise to query-time attention routing, while the correct binding stays decodable and causally effective.
