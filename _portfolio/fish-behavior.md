---
title: "Zebrafish tracking"
order: 2
excerpt: "Software that tracks zebrafish in video and measures how a group swims together, plus simulations that reproduce what the fish do."
---

This software follows each fish in a video and measures how far apart the fish
swim, how fast they move, how they turn, and how much time they spend near the
tank walls. Simulated fish, each following simple rules, then reproduce the
group's behaviour.

The tracker replaced scoring by hand in the Neuro-ethology Lab at Ashoka
University. It runs alongside a closed-loop virtual-reality setup for zebrafish
that I also built.
