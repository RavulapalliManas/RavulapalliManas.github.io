Research positions

2025 – now

Perseus, remote. Chief Scientist.

I set the research agenda and supervise a small research team. The work covers how language models gain and lose skills during training, interpretability, and evaluation.

2025 – now

Ashoka University, Sonipat. Thesis research.

How recurrent networks that predict their next observation learn maps of space. I built the analysis tools that measure the structure of those maps, and I test them across ten simulated environments.

2025

European Molecular Biology Laboratory, Heidelberg. Summer student, July.

I used optical tweezers to study how transcription factors find specific DNA sequences. I also worked with light-sheet and confocal microscopes and with 3D reconstruction of microscopy images.

2025

Neural Systems Lab, University of Hyderabad. Summer research assistant, June – August.

I set up and ran insect electrophysiology recordings, and traced nerve pathways in grasshoppers. I designed behaviour experiments in weaver ants, including mirror-recognition tests.

2024 – now

Neuro-ethology Lab, Ashoka University. Research assistant.

I built the lab's automated zebrafish tracker and its closed-loop virtual-reality setup. I am developing a test of number sense in zebrafish, and I wrote the lab's recording and analysis protocols.
