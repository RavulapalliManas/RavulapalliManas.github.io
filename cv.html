Research experience

Perseus

Remote

Chief Scientist. 2025 to present.

  • Set and lead the company's research agenda across four lines: world models for code, developmental and continued pretraining, mechanistic interpretability, and evaluation. Supervise a small research team and external collaborations.
  • Coding world models. Leading work on learned models of a codebase and its execution behaviour that an agent can roll forward and plan against, rather than retrieving text. Related architectural work adapts TEM-style structural priors as a persistent, slot-structured state on a transformer backbone.
  • Developmental pretraining. Built the experimental programme on when capabilities become acquirable, retainable, and recoverable during training, from a small controlled model up to OLMo-2 continued pretraining, under a preregistration protocol with predictions frozen before launch.
  • Interpretability. Built the instrument stack for the routing and binding programme: Jacobian-based binding probes, path patching, per-checkpoint developmental sweeps. Ran an adversarial audit of our own corpus that retracted several previously published numbers.
  • Evaluation. Direct BioBench, an external-facing benchmark for biological mechanism reasoning built on a symbolic verifier that certifies or rejects a chain of reasoning rather than scoring accuracy. Developed with external research partners including ARC.

Independent Research (ISM Thesis), Ashoka University

Sonipat, HR

Predictive RNNs and cognitive map formation. August 2025 to present.

  • Implemented the Levenstein et al. (2024) sequential predictive RNN, with NormReLU, rollout k = 5, and RMSProp under per-parameter learning-rate scaling, to test how environment statistics drive map formation.
  • Built diagnostics for representational structure: occupancy-normalised mutual information, geodesic sRSA via Dijkstra for non-convex arenas, participation ratio, RDM Frobenius delta, and persistent homology via Ripser.
  • Tested a topology-before-geometry hypothesis, whether correct Betti numbers emerge before metric structure, across ten environments varying geometry, topology, scale, and aliasing, in RatInABox and MiniGrid.
