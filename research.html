Thesis

Independent Study Module, Ashoka University

Cognitive maps are symmetry quotients: why place fields repeat in identical rooms. Supervised by Prof. Debayan Gupta, August 2025 to present.

The module implements the Levenstein et al. (2024) sequential predictive RNN, with NormReLU, rollout k = 5, and RMSProp under per-parameter learning-rate scaling, to test how environment statistics drive map formation.

The diagnostics for representational structure are occupancy-normalised mutual information, geodesic sRSA computed via Dijkstra for non-convex arenas, participation ratio, RDM Frobenius delta, and persistent homology via Ripser.

The topology-before-geometry hypothesis asks whether correct Betti numbers emerge before metric structure. It is tested across ten environments that vary geometry, topology, scale, and aliasing, in RatInABox and MiniGrid.
